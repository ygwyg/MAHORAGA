Market basics

Trading is decision-making under uncertainty.

A trade is not a prediction. It is a bet with an entry, size, invalidation point, exit plan, and expected payoff. The professional habit is to define risk before you chase reward.

Vocabulary anchor

TickerA short symbol for an asset, like AAPL, SPY, or BTC/USD.
PositionWhat you currently own or owe in the market.
SpreadThe gap between buyer bid and seller ask.
P&LProfit and loss, realized or unrealized.

The market object model

Every trade starts with an asset and an order.

Asset

A thing you can trade: stock, ETF, option contract, or crypto pair. Each asset has eligibility rules, trading hours, and liquidity.

Order

An instruction to buy or sell. The order defines side, quantity, order type, time in force, and sometimes limit or stop prices.

Fill

The broker confirms that part or all of your order executed. Paper fills are simulated; live fills interact with real market liquidity.

Risk

The amount you can lose if the idea is wrong. Good systems cap risk before they allow a trade.

Order types

How buying and selling actually works.

Markets are auctions. Buyers place bids, sellers place asks, and orders match when prices cross.

OrderWhat it meansWhen beginners use itMain risk
Market Buy or sell immediately at the best available market price. Highly liquid assets where immediate execution matters. You do not control the exact price.
Limit Buy no higher than your limit or sell no lower than your limit. When price discipline matters more than speed. The order may not fill.
Stop Trigger a market order after a stop price is reached. Risk exits and downside protection. Fast moves can fill worse than expected.
Stop limit Trigger a limit order after a stop price is reached. Controlled exits in normal markets. The limit may prevent an exit during a sharp move.
Bracket Entry with linked take-profit and stop-loss exits. Automated risk management for a planned trade. Bad parameters can exit too early or too late.

Strategy basics

A strategy is a rule set, not a vibe.

Universe

What can the system trade? Example: only liquid large-cap stocks and major ETFs.

Signal

What suggests opportunity? Example: catalyst news plus volume spike plus trend.

Entry

What exact condition allows a buy or sell?

Exit

What exact condition locks profit, cuts loss, or times out?

Review

Did the thesis, timing, sizing, and execution behave as expected?

Watchlists

A watchlist is a small universe of symbols you care about. It reduces cognitive load. Instead of asking "what should we trade anywhere?", you ask "which of these known symbols meets our rules today?"

Options

Options are contracts whose value depends on an underlying asset. A call benefits from upside; a put benefits from downside. They can lose value quickly due to time decay and volatility changes, so options should remain disabled until the stock strategy is observable.

The beginner checklist

Before any bot trades, answer these questions.

  • ✓What symbols is the strategy allowed to trade?
  • ✓How much can one trade lose if the idea is wrong?
  • ✓How much can the whole day lose before the kill switch activates?
  • ✓What data made the system enter, and what data will make it exit?
  • ✓Can you replay the decision tomorrow and understand it without the code?
