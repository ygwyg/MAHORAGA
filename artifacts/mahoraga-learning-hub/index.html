Mental model

There are three systems you are learning at once.

The fastest way to become competent is to separate the domains. Trading teaches what decisions mean. Alpaca teaches how those decisions become API orders. MAHORAGA teaches how automation turns data into controlled actions.

Open system guide

How the bot thinks

The trading loop is a pipeline, not magic.

1. Observe

Gather market data, news, social signals, current positions, and account risk state.

2. Research

Use the LLM to summarize catalysts, risk, context, and confidence for candidate symbols.

3. Decide

Rules decide whether a candidate is tradeable, sized correctly, and allowed by policy.

4. Execute

Orders are sent to Alpaca paper trading first; fills and open orders are monitored.

5. Learn

Every decision should be journaled so you can compare thesis, execution, and outcome.

Recommended sequence

Your next 30 days should be boring on purpose.

The business outcome we want is confidence, not action. A controlled paper phase lets you build data, learn the product, and avoid paying tuition to the market before the system is observable.

Days 1-3

Get the project healthy

Use the forked branch, fix LLM deployment configuration, deploy Worker and dashboard, keep Alpaca in paper mode, and verify the dashboard reads Worker JSON.

Days 4-10

Run observation-only

Let MAHORAGA collect events and research signals while order placement remains disabled or capped to tiny paper trades.

Days 11-20

Measure paper trades

Turn on paper orders with strict daily loss, position, and asset limits. Review every fill like a product analytics event.

Days 21-30

Choose one extension

Do not add ten strategies. Add one measurable improvement: better journaling, a safer risk gate, a single new signal, or a cleaner dashboard review flow.

Important safety stance

Keep this on Alpaca paper trading until you can explain every order MAHORAGA places, why it placed it, how much it could lose, and what condition would make it exit.

Revenue path

Treat revenue as a research product: build a repeatable strategy, quantify edge, reduce drawdowns, and only then consider live capital or a paid analytics/product layer around the system.
